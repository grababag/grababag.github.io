Who We Are

Your Local Waste Removal Team

Grab A Bag is a Dartford-based skip bag and mini skip hire company serving homeowners, landlords and tradespeople across Kent. Founded with a simple mission — to make waste removal easy, affordable and reliable — we've become the go-to choice for customers who want a local company they can actually trust.

We're proud to be one of the only businesses in the area offering skip bags alongside traditional mini skip hire, giving our customers more choice and flexibility than anyone else locally.

Whether you're clearing out a garage, renovating a bathroom or managing a building project, we'll make sure your waste is dealt with quickly and responsibly — at a price that won't break the bank.

Why Trust Us

Experience, Expertise & Local Knowledge

We're real people, based locally, who understand the waste removal needs of Kent residents and businesses.

Local Expertise

We know Dartford and Kent inside out. Our local knowledge means faster deliveries, better routing and a more personal service than you'd get from a national chain.

Real Relationships

When you call us, you're talking to Dean directly — not a call centre. We build real relationships with our customers and many choose us again and again for that reason.

Eco-Responsible
