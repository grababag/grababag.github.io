Our Services — Mini Skip Hire

When you need more capacity than a bag can offer, our mini skip hire service is the answer. We supply all sizes of mini skip from 2 yards to 6 yards, covering most parts of Kent — with competitive prices starting from just £120.

Flexible Hire Periods

We offer both short-term and long-term skip hire. Just let us know how long you need the skip when you enquire and one of our friendly team will be happy to accommodate your schedule.

What Can Go in a Mini Skip?

Our mini skips accept most general, mixed, and builders' waste. The same restrictions apply as our skip bags — we cannot accept asbestos, gas bottles, fridges, or other hazardous materials.

Please note: We cannot accept asbestos, gas bottles, fridges, freezers, tyres, or any other hazardous materials. If you are unsure whether your waste is accepted, please get in touch and we will be happy to advise.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit for a skip on the road?

If the skip is placed on a public highway or pavement, you'll need a permit from your local council. Skips placed on private driveways don't need a permit.

How quickly can you deliver?

We aim to deliver as quickly as possible, often same or next day for customers in the Dartford area.

Can I extend my hire period?

Yes — just get in touch and we'll sort it out. We understand building projects can overrun.
