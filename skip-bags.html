About Our Skip Bags

Our skip bags — available in mini and midi sizes — offer a convenient, cost-effective alternative to traditional skips. Ideal for homeowners, landlords and small tradespeople who need a flexible, no-fuss waste solution.

As the only local business in the Dartford area providing skip bags, we pride ourselves on speedy delivery and collection. Simply fill the bag with your waste and let us know when you're ready — we'll come and collect it.

Why Choose A Skip Bag?

Unlike a traditional skip, our bags don't require a council permit if placed on your own driveway. They're also more compact, fitting neatly on most residential drives. No barriers, no signage — just a convenient bag that gets the job done.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can go in a skip bag?

Most general, mixed and builders' waste. We cannot accept asbestos, gas bottles, fridges, or other hazardous materials.

How long can I keep the bag?

Keep it as long as you need. Just call us when you're ready for collection.

Do I need a permit for a skip bag?

No — if placed on your own driveway or private land, no permit is needed.

What's the difference between a mini bag and a midi bag?

The mini bag is perfect for small clearances. The midi bag holds more and is ideal for bigger projects.
